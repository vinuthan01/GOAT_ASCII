01 / Drop

Every pixelgets a letter.

GOAT = Greatest Of All TimeASCII = American Standard Code for Information Interchange

Drop in a photo or a video and GOAT ASCII redraws it out of plain text, keeping the colors your picture already had. Dark areas stay empty, bright ones glow. It all runs inside this tab, so your file never leaves your device.

Drop a photo or video here

Or pick one from your files, paste with Ctrl+V, or go live with your camera.

JPG, PNG, WEBP, MP4 or WEBM Stays on this device. Nothing is uploaded.

03 / How

How a picture becomes letters

No AI model, no server. Just four small steps repeated on every frame, about thirty times a second.

  1. Shrink it

    Your image is squeezed down to a grid with one tiny cell for every character that will appear on screen.

  2. Measure it

    Each cell gets a brightness score. Edges score extra, so outlines stay sharp instead of melting into mush.

  3. Pick a letter

    Dim cells get a light touch like a dot or a comma. Bright, busy cells get a heavy hitter like @ or $. Pure black gets nothing at all.

  4. Paint it

    Every letter takes the color of the cell it came from, then a soft glow is added so the whole thing looks lit from behind.

04 / Features

Made to be used, not just looked at

Open any file and these are waiting in the viewer.

Before and after

Turn on Compare and drag the cyan line to slide between your original and the ASCII version. Press B to toggle it.

Export presets

Pick Quick share, Standard or Crisp. You see the resolution, frame rate and an estimated file size before you record, and a progress bar while it runs.

Styles

Original colors are always the default. Switch to White on black, Black on white, Phosphor, Amber, Neon duotone, Violet, Ice, Sunset, Rose, Crimson or Rainbow when you want a mood. Press S to cycle.

Keyboard first

Space play or pause, ←→ seek, ↑↓ detail, F full screen, ? for the full list.

Works on your phone

Big touch targets, a draggable compare line and controls that wrap instead of overflowing.

Private by design

Your file is read by your browser and drawn on a canvas. There is no upload step, no account and no tracking.

Webcam mode

Press Use webcam and your camera turns into live ASCII. Nothing is recorded or sent anywhere.

Tune the look

Swap letters for blocks, braille dots, binary or your own word. Adjust brightness, contrast and invert. Your choices are remembered.

Copy as text

Copy the art as plain text, or save a .txt file for chats and bios. Press Ctrl+V to paste a picture straight in.

Install it

Works offline after the first visit and can be installed like an app from your browser menu.
